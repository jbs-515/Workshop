Here I set some tips I consider usefull for my work on the workshop ,

The soldering iron tips i feel more comfortable working with are the ones of JLC set on the [TOOL List]
This variety of JBC tips compose a wide range of aplications.

Usually for non lead tin the temperature i should reach with the soldering stationin no more than 370ºC. Usually valid for SMD and through hole solders. 
And for the heatgun, 330ºC with 10% air flow should sufece to melt the soldering paste and to not set the components flying acorss the PCB.

> [!TIP]
> For sensitive components with 0.1% errors like resistors for example, it si recomended to not excede this temperature of 370ªC or even set it lower so this high heat applied on the component does not degrade the functionallity of the element.

To avoid "Cold Soldering" apply enough heat, flux to distribute it evenly, and retire the soldering tip slowly from the zone.

Always check components before and after soldering/desoldering
